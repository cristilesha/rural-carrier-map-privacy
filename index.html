Rural Carrier Map

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026. Rural Carrier Map helps carriers make and follow routes, manage stops, and talk with other carriers. This policy explains the information the app uses.

Information you enter

You can enter route names, delivery addresses, mailbox and stop locations, package information, profile information, and notes. You can add a profile photo, scan route documents or package barcodes, send chat messages, and choose to share a route with another carrier in a private conversation.

Location and work area

With your permission, the app uses device location to show your position, record or follow a line of travel, and show nearby stops. Carrier chat uses the approximate location of your work ZIP code to find messages within 40 miles. Nearby chat does not show your precise GPS location to other carriers.

Storage and sharing

Your routes and delivery information are stored on your device unless you choose to send a route through private chat or another sharing feature. Carrier account details, work ZIP and its approximate coordinates, nearby messages, private messages, privately shared routes, blocks, and reports use Firebase services to provide chat. Nearby messages are visible to signed-in carriers in the relevant area. Private conversations and routes sent there are available to the participants. Google Maps provides map imagery and related mapping features.

These services may process information as described in their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices and deletion

You can change location and camera permissions in iPhone Settings. In chat you can delete messages you sent, block other carriers, and report content. To delete your carrier chat account, open Nearby Carrier Chat → Account & support → Delete carrier chat account and confirm with your password. This removes your chat profile and messages you sent. Routes stored on your phone remain there until you delete those routes or remove the app. Safety reports may be retained as needed to investigate abuse.

If account deletion fails, email ruralcarriermaps@gmail.com for help.

Questions

For privacy questions or support, email ruralcarriermaps@gmail.com.
